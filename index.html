About RMO Consultancy

Assurance you can build your future on.

RMO Consultancy is based in Nairobi and specialises in internal audit, enterprise risk, governance, regulatory compliance, and data protection. The firm was founded and is led by Robert Ombego.

Robert brings over 7 years of specialised expertise in internal audit, risk management, and forensics, and personally leads engagements.

His Master's in Cybersecurity & Digital Forensics and ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor certification connect directly to data protection, information security management, privacy compliance, and digital trust.

Data Protection & Privacy Compliance

Helping organisations meet their data protection obligations and turn privacy into demonstrable digital trust — from statutory registration to board-level accountability.

Kenya Data Protection Act & ODPC

GDPR Readiness

DPIAs
